About

Hey, my name is Fabrice.

I’m a designer focused on creating thoughtful digital experiences and visual identities. My work combines clarity, functionality, and bold aesthetics to create experiences that are both intuitive and distinctive.

Alongside my independent work, I’m part of the team behind White Risk, Switzerland’s leading avalanche and ski touring planning app. I lead the app’s visual identity and design system while shaping the user experience to make complex information easy to understand and navigate.

Whether I’m designing a digital product, building a brand identity, or refining a design system, I believe great design starts with understanding people and solving real problems. My approach is collaborative, strategic, and detail-oriented, with a strong focus on creating work that feels effortless to use and built to last.

I work directly with my clients and stay closely involved throughout every project, from concept to final delivery. No layers. Just thoughtful design.
