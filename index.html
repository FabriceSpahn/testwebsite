About

I’m a designer focused on creating thoughtful digital experiences and visual identities. My work combines clarity, functionality, and bold aesthetics to craft work that is both intuitive and distinctive.

Alongside my independent work, I’m currently part of the team behind White Risk, Switzerland’s leading avalanche and ski touring planning app. I’m responsible for the app’s visual identity and design system, while also shaping the user experience and user flows to make complex information accessible and easy to navigate.

I work independently and stay closely involved in every project from concept to final delivery. No layers. Just thoughtful design.
